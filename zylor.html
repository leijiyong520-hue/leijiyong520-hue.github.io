  • Who We Are
    ZYLOR is a system-driven autonomous cleaning solutions provider, focused on enabling scalable, real-world deployment of robotic cleaning across commercial, industrial, and infrastructure environments.
     
    We are a core brand under Ningbo GLGW ENERGY STORAGE Co., Ltd. (GLGW) — a leading digital industrial platform with over 20 subsidiaries, a global service network, and extensive experience supporting large-scale operations across the Belt and Road markets. This foundation provides ZYLOR with strong supply chain capabilities, engineering resources, and global delivery support.
  • What We Do
    ZYLOR goes beyond individual machines. We design and deliver integrated cleaning systems that combine robotics, workflow design, and operational logic — enabling consistent, data-driven cleaning in complex, real-world environments.

    Our solutions are built to move from initial deployment to scalable operations, supporting facilities such as shopping centers, office portfolios, industrial sites, transportation hubs, and large outdoor spaces.
  • Who We Work With
    We collaborate with a wide range of partners and clients, including:
    Local distributors expanding into automation solutions; System integrators and solution providers; Facility service providers managing daily operations; Enterprise groups operating multi-site portfolios
  • How We Work
    We work with our partners and clients through a structured, long-term collaboration model — not just delivering technology, but enabling operational success.
     
    Our approach typically follows a scalable path:
    Starting with focused deployments in real environments; Expanding through pilot projects and validated use cases; Scaling across multiple sites with standardized systems.
     
    Throughout this process, ZYLOR provides system design, deployment support, training, and ongoing optimization — ensuring that autonomous cleaning becomes a reliable, repeatable part of daily operations.
