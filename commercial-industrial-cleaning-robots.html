Frequently Asked Questions About Commercial & Industrial Cleaning Robots

How Do I Choose the Right Cleaning Robot for My Facility?

The right cleaning robot depends on your floor type, cleaning area, floor conditions, and daily cleaning requirements. ZYLOR offers commercial and industrial cleaning robots for different cleaning tasks. Share your facility details with our team to identify suitable models.

What Is the Difference Between Commercial and Industrial Cleaning Robots?

Commercial cleaning robots are generally intended for environments such as shopping malls, hotels, offices, and other commercial facilities. Industrial cleaning robots are designed for cleaning requirements in factories, workshops, and warehouses. The right choice depends on the cleaning task, floor conditions, facility layout, and operating requirements.

What Cleaning Functions Do ZYLOR Robots Support?

ZYLOR offers different models for tasks such as floor scrubbing, industrial sweeping, and specialized carpet steam cleaning and drying. Available functions vary by model. Review the individual product specifications to confirm which cleaning methods each robot supports.

Can Cleaning Robots Work in Different Facility Layouts?

Different robot models are designed for different operating environments. Floor space, aisle width, obstacles, floor conditions, and pedestrian traffic should be considered before selecting a robot. We recommend evaluating your facility requirements to determine which model is suitable.

What Information Should I Provide When Requesting a Quote?

To help us recommend suitable options, please provide your facility type, floor material, approximate cleaning area, cleaning tasks, and operating requirements. You can also share your target model, destination country, and project timeline if available. Our team can then review your requirements and discuss the next steps.

How Much Does a Commercial or Industrial Cleaning Robot Cost?

The price of a commercial or industrial cleaning robot depends on the model, cleaning functions, configuration, and project requirements. ZYLOR offers different cleaning robot options for offices, shopping malls, factories, and warehouses. Contact our team with your cleaning area, floor conditions, and application needs to receive a suitable product recommendation and quotation.

Does ZYLOR Provide Deployment and Technical Support for Cleaning Robots?

Yes. ZYLOR provides product selection assistance and technical consultation to help businesses plan their cleaning robot projects. Depending on the selected model and project requirements, our team can discuss deployment planning, operating guidance, and available after-sales support to help customers integrate cleaning robots into their facilities.
